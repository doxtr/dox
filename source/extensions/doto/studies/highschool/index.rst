High School
===========

Subject notes and task repositories from secondary school. Each subject is
its own *doto repository* (``.doto.toml`` + ``.doto/tasks.json``) with its own
task-ID prefix, e.g. a task in ``maths/`` becomes ``MATHS-001``.
